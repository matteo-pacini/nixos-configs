# The tour's captions, by scene

The owner's wording for each scene of tour.steps, in order. `desk-nest record`
reads the timed copies of these lines, the `caption` steps in tour.steps; edit
both together (the desk-nest check fails when a caption here is not in
tour.steps, or the scenes differ).

## bar
A showcase of my custom Quickshell setup on Hyprland
The bar: workspaces, the clock with the next event, and one item per panel

## launcher
The launcher: type to filter, Enter to start

## notifications
Notifications stack at the top right, and clear with one key

## calendar
Under the clock: the month and today's events

## calendar-full
